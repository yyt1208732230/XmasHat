Competition rules

Make and submit

Enter as many designs as you like, in your own time. Upload a still JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 3 MB. Videos and animated images are out. The title and description are optional.

Vote and score

Give one vote per category on as many entries as you like. Tap again to remove it. Total score = ✨ Wow factor votes + 🎯 Theme fit votes.

Vote totals and rankings are live. Other participants cannot see who voted. The winner earns an award. Earlier entries appear first when scores tie, and tied leaders share the win.

Keep it fair and respectful

Create one account for yourself. Use a password just for this competition. If you forget it, your account becomes a ghost account 👻. The site and competition data will be removed after the event.

Entries must respect racial and gender equality and diverse beliefs, and follow QUT’s student and staff codes of conduct.
