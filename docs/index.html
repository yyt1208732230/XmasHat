Competition rules

Make and submit

  • Enter as many designs as you like, in your own time.
  • Upload one still JPG, PNG or WebP image, up to 3 MB.
  • Videos and animated images are not accepted.
  • Title and description are optional.

Vote and score

  • Vote once per category on as many designs as you like.
  • Tap a vote again to remove it.
  • Total score = ✨ Wow factor votes + 🎯 Theme fit votes.
  • Live totals and rankings are public. Voter identities stay private.
  • The winner earns an award. Earlier entries rank higher in a tie. Tied leaders share the win.

Keep it fair and respectful

  • Create one account for yourself.
  • Use a password just for this competition. If you forget it, your account becomes a ghost account 👻.
  • The site and competition data will be removed after the event.
  • Respect racial and gender equality and diverse beliefs.
  • Follow QUT’s student and staff codes of conduct.
